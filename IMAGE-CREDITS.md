The photographic prototype uses remotely hosted images so that the layout can be evaluated immediately.

The Unsplash assets are used under the Unsplash licence. The gibbon image is available through Wikimedia Commons; retain the photographer credit and verify the file-page licence before final publication.

For the production site, download approved copies into `images/` and replace the remote URLs in `styles.css`. Your own field footage, app recordings and research figures should ultimately replace generic stock photography where available.
